# QD-1 / QS-1 phase acceptance checklist

This checklist tracks mandatory engineering gates against recorded evidence. A scoped pass does not close either phase. Expanded engineering is limited to `BINANCE:BTCUSDT` Spot 1m, SPT Custom, and `paper-close-v1`; other timeframes and the `paper-close-cost-v2` execution model remain denied until their own parity evidence exists. Additive V2 capacity contracts do not grant execution-model parity. Production admission remains at 10K bars. No expanded capability or production deployment is accepted here.
